Who We Are

Driven by the Timeless Principle of "Jeev Daya"

"Jeev Daya" represents the profound ethical principle of extending reverence, mercy, and active protection to every living being.

Founded by veterinary doctors, student advocates, and passionate animal lovers, our mission is to eliminate needless suffering on our streets through proactive rescue, sterilisation, medical care, and public education.
