Our Story & Heritage

About Jeev Daya

Rooted in the timeless Indian value of "Jeev Daya" (compassion for all living beings), we are on a mission to build a society where no animal suffers in silence.

Our Mission

To provide rapid, 24x7 emergency medical rescue, trauma surgery, and rehabilitation to street animals, stray cattle, and distressed birds. We bridge the gap between people on the ground and certified veterinary care.

Our Vision

A compassionate world where every stray animal lives free of human cruelty, traffic abandonment, and preventable illness. We strive to empower every neighborhood with animal first-aid knowledge and empathy.

Our Pillars

How We Protect Animal Life

Our integrated model ensures animals get immediate rescue, world-class medical care, and permanent safety.

24x7 Rapid Ambulance Fleet

Equipped with stretchers, oxygen support, splints, and trained handlers to safely transport critical accident cases.

Free Stray OPD & Surgery

Partnered veterinary clinics conduct free orthopedic surgeries, tumor removals, and treatments for un-owned animals.

Humane Rabies Eradication

Routine rabies vaccination drives and Animal Birth Control (ABC) sterilizations to create healthy, peaceful canine communities.

Shelter & Foster Homes

Safe sanctuary for amputee animals, blind cows, and newborn orphans who cannot safely return to the streets.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common queries regarding rescue protocols, volunteering, and donations.

Does Jeev Daya charge any fee for rescuing a stray animal?

No, never. All emergency rescues, surgeries, and treatments for un-owned street animals are 100% free of cost, funded purely through generous community donations.

What should I do after submitting an online rescue report?

Once submitted, our dispatch system assigns your report a Ticket ID. Please keep your phone accessible. If possible, stay at a safe distance near the animal until the ambulance arrives so they don't wander off.

Can I adopt an animal after they recover?

Absolutely! In our Rescue Cases section, animals marked with the green "Ready For Adoption" badge are vaccinated, dewormed, and eager for loving forever homes.

Are donations eligible for tax exemption in India?

Yes. Jeev Daya is a registered charitable trust. All donations are 50% tax-exempt under Section 80G of the Indian Income Tax Act. A digital tax receipt is issued instantly upon donation.
