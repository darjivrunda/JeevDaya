While You Wait For The Rescuer

  • Stay at a Safe Distance: In pain, animals may bite or scratch out of fear.
  • Do NOT Feed Milk or Solid Food: Feeding an injured or unconscious animal can cause fatal choking. Clean water in a shallow bowl is fine.
  • Provide Shade or Shelter: If on hot asphalt or under rain, place a cardboard shield or umbrella over them.
  • Keep an Eye on Them: Frightened animals may try to drag themselves into traffic or hiding spots.
